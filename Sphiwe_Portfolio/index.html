WHO AM I?

Hey — I'm Sphiwe Dube, a second-year Diploma in Computer Science student at Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) in Pretoria, South Africa.

I'm obsessed with cybersecurity — understanding how systems break, and more importantly how to build them so they don't. Whether it's analysing network traffic, writing secure Java, or designing tamper-evident systems, I'm always looking for the security angle.

Outside the classroom I chase certifications, build projects, enter hackathons, and explore the world of ethical hacking. My mission is to become a cybersecurity analyst who protects South African businesses and communities.
